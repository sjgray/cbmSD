cbmSD-pass - Pass-thru board for cbmSD-xd
=========================================

**NEW** Webpage: http://cbmsteve.ca/cbmsd/index.html

These pass-thru boards plug into the back of the PET/CBM and
connects to power (via cassette port or power adapter).
The "XD" connector combines the power and IEEE signals into one
proprietary pinout for convenient connection to the cbmSD-xd
board using a simple 20-pin ribbon cable. Note: That the IEEE
standard is a 24-pin connector. The GROUND pins have been
consolidated and 5V pins have been added.

You will need a simple cassette port power plug or you can
power it with a standard 5V adapter with barrel jack.

There are two versions of the board:

1) With IEEE PET-Edge connector (like on most PET/CBM).
   Horizontal or Vertical mount options.

2) With IEEE (Centronics 24-pin) connector as used on devices.


Features
--------

- Proprietary 20-pin "cbmSD" header (IEEE + Power).
- Barrel Jack for 5V power.
- Power LED.
- Passthru connector to additional devices.
